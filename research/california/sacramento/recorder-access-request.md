# Recorder index access request

Submit through the [Sacramento County Clerk/Recorder email request form](https://ccr.saccounty.gov/us/en/email-request-form.html). Keep the confirmation and response in the project evidence log; do not commit any delivered record data to Git.

## Suggested subject

Availability of Recorder General Index data for research

## Message

Hello,

I’m researching a small internal parcel-data pilot using Sacramento County public records. I understand that the online recorded-document index is an electronic representation of the Recorder’s General Index and is generally updated within two business days.

Does the Clerk/Recorder offer an official bulk file, subscription, licensed export, API, or public-record request process for General Index metadata? I’m interested in index fields such as recording date, grantor and grantee names, document title or type, document number, book, and page. I am not requesting document images at this stage.

If an approved data-access option exists, could you share its available years, fields, file format, update schedule, cost, access requirements, and terms for internal storage, automated processing, derived data, and redistribution? Please also let me know whether there are rate limits or other restrictions on the public online index.

Thank you,

Gabriel Harris
