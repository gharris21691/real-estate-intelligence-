# San Bernardino ATC open-data request

## Submission route

- Submit through the [Auditor-Controller/Treasurer/Tax Collector Contact Us form](https://www.sbcountyatc.gov/contact-us).
- Select Tax Collector, Property Tax, Open Data, or the closest available category.
- If the form does not route open-data questions, call (909) 387-8308 and ask which team owns the ATC OpenData catalog.

## Request

**Subject:** Permission question for scheduled use of ATC OpenData

Hello,

I’m evaluating three datasets from the San Bernardino County ATC OpenData site for a real-estate research project:

- Defaulted Bills 5 Years or Newer
- Bills and Installments 5 Years or Newer
- Bills and Installments Current Year

The catalog provides CSV and Parquet downloads and says the files update weekly, but the license field is blank. May we download these files automatically once a week for internal commercial research, retain dated source snapshots, and create internal derived indicators from the data?

We would not republish the source records. Our first pass would also exclude escrow-company and transfer-destination fields. Could you confirm any rate limits, attribution requirements, retention limits, or other conditions we should follow?

One technical question: does `Account Number` map directly to the Assessor’s Parcel Number, or is there an official crosswalk we should use?

Thank you,

Gabriel Harris
