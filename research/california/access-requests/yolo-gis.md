## Submission route

- Email `GIS@yolocounty.org`.

## Request

**Subject:** Automated-use terms for Yolo County public parcel service

Hello Yolo County GIS team,

I’m evaluating the county’s `Public/Parcels_Public` FeatureServer for an internal real-estate research project. May we query the service automatically once a week, page through results using the published ArcGIS REST interface, retain dated snapshots, and create internal derived tables for commercial research?

The first phase would use parcel identifiers, assessed land and structure values, tax-rate area, land-use code and description, acreage, revision date, jurisdiction, and geometry. We would exclude the `Name` field and every situs-address field, and we would not republish the county's source records.

We also understand that the separate Official Records Search prohibits scripted or unattended access. We will not automate that recorder utility.

Could you confirm any request-frequency or concurrency limits, retention rules, attribution requirements, redistribution restrictions, or preferred method for learning about service changes?

Thank you,

Gabriel Harris
