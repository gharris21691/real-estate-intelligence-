# Source Research

Research files document how public records can be accessed and whether they are suitable for a future, compliant pilot. They do not authorize collection.

## Evidence standard

Use official agency pages, published technical documentation, fee schedules, data dictionaries, terms, and direct agency responses. Record the page title, URL, verification date, and the specific claim supported. Mark uncertain claims as questions.

## Workflow

1. Create or update the county entry in `california/county-catalog.md`.
2. Complete the county's source-research specification.
3. Evaluate each source family in its own worksheet.
4. Review privacy, terms, cost, reliability, and identifier compatibility.
5. Record a decision: `Verified`, `Approved for pilot`, `Blocked`, or `Deferred`.

Status describes evidence maturity, not data availability.
