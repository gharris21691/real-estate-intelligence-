# Recorder expansion gate

Status: `Deferred pending official access response and Assessor transfer-file sample`

## Question

After the base parcel pilot is validated, can recent recorded transfer metadata add reliable, explainable transaction context without relying on portal scraping or ambiguous party matching?

## Preferred source order

1. Assessor two-year Change in Ownership transfer file, if its parcel linkage, coverage, and terms are acceptable.
2. Recorder-provided bulk General Index data, subscription, licensed export, or API.
3. Manual Recorder index checks for case-specific validation only.

The public Recorder search portal is not an acquisition option unless the Clerk/Recorder explicitly approves automated use.

## Minimum fields to evaluate

- parcel identifier when provided;
- recording date;
- document number;
- document type/title;
- book and page when applicable;
- grantor/grantee roles only if approved and necessary; and
- source update/effective date.

Document images are outside the proposed expansion.

## Approval criteria

- The source owner confirms the access channel and permitted automated use.
- Parcel linkage is direct or has a documented review process; names alone do not create a parcel match.
- Coverage, lag, corrections, cancellations, and document-type definitions are documented.
- Party data is minimized, access-controlled, and retained only for an approved purpose.
- The source meets the provenance and reconciliation guardrails in the Sacramento acceptance plan.

## Stop conditions

- Only portal scraping is available.
- Rights to store or process the data remain unclear.
- Transfer events cannot be linked without high-risk person matching.
- The source adds no material analyst value beyond the secured roll and parcel geometry.
